Business model

Free for retailers. Four revenue streams.

Stream 01

Consumer convenience fee

A small fee baked into the checkout price. Invisible to the consumer. Meaningful at scale.

Volume engine · always-on

Stream 02

Deal of the Day

Retailers pay for the premium top slot inside their category.

£[TBC] per day · per category

Stream 03

Deal Alerts

Pay to push a promotional notification to opted-in consumers in your category.

£1 per alert

Stream 04

Geo-location Alerts

Auto-triggered alerts when an opted-in consumer walks within range of your shop.

£1 per push

The platform is free to join. Retailers only spend when it's working.
