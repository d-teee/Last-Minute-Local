Why we exist

Independent businesses lose money every single hour, quietly.

An empty barber's chair at 3pm. A half-full yoga class. A table for two nobody booked. That time, and the revenue in it, vanishes the second it passes, with no way to get it back.

Time-bound inventory has no second chance

Unlike products on a shelf, a missed appointment or unsold seating can't be sold tomorrow. Once the clock passes, that value is gone for good.

Small businesses can't compete on visibility

Big chains run promotions with dedicated marketing teams. The independent shop on the corner has never had an easy way to say "we're quiet right now, come save."

Customers want spontaneity, not spam

People already act fast on time-limited local deals. They just need them delivered at the right moment, for the right category, without noise.

The gap has simply never been filled

No platform today matches real-time local availability with real-time local demand. We built one, free for both sides to use.

The AI

Our AI sets the price. Not guesswork, not panic.

The moment a slot is about to go to waste, our pricing engine starts lifting the discount in real time, calibrated to exactly how much time is left and how much interest the offer is getting. Retailers set the floor they're comfortable with. The AI never crosses it. It just finds the smallest discount that still fills the seat before the clock runs out.
